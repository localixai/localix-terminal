Demo sites

Eight small sites to open in the llx-desk window by their path, with no server to start. Each holds the same data on every load, so a task on it has one right answer.

llx-desk window '{"action": "open", "kind": "browser", "url": "demo/shop.html"}'
  • Shop

    A plant shop with a catalog, a cart and a promo code.

    Search, filter chips, sorting, quantity steppers, a drawer, a total computed

  • Todos

    A list for the week: add, finish, rename and remove.

    Enter to add, checkboxes, editing in place, filters, a counter

  • Orders

    Sixty orders in a table, by page.

    Search, a status filter, sortable columns, pagination, a details dialog

  • Booking

    Nights at a mountain hut, picked on a calendar.

    A date range on a custom calendar, months, nights not free, guests, a price

  • Onboarding

    A workspace set up in steps, some only for teams.

    A multi-step form, fields that depend on earlier answers, a review, going back

  • Feed

    Trail reports that load as you scroll.

    Skeletons, infinite scroll, new posts arriving, likes

  • Settings

    Account settings on four tabs.

    Tabs, switches, a segmented control, a slider, a combobox, typing to confirm

  • 2048

    Slide numbered tiles until two of a kind join.

    Arrow keys on the page, a board that changes with every key, planning ahead
